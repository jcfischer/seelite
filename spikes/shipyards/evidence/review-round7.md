## O2 — roof correction required

Portal layering and exterior radiator details improve the industrial read. However, the new roof panels stand high above the hull as steep paired pitched canopies with large black triangular cheeks. In the hero view they resemble shed roofs or open lids. The target uses shallow, integrated, armored slopes that follow the body envelope; it does not have these tall separated roof structures. This lowers composition despite the detail improvement.

Minimum bounded correction: lower and flatten the new panels into shallow sloped armor integrated with the existing roof height. Keep the recessed service channels, but make them narrow and mechanically lined; eliminate the large exposed triangular voids/cheeks. A modest slope, approximately 8–12 degrees, with a thin dark inset beneath the armor is a better starting point than the current tall wedge. Add visible seams or a small grille to these broad pale faces so they do not become featureless sheets. Preserve all portal, craft, manipulator, and capsule changes. Rejudge O2 only after that correction; do not assume an automatic pass.
