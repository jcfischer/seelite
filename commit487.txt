Measure the clearance behind "nothing in front of it" (#487)

`sky-only` has captioned itself since #104 as "The backdrop with nothing in
front of it". The claim is about the picture, and `__emptiestDirection`
guarantees only a ranking: it takes the maximum of the *minimum* angle over 512
candidate directions, which returns the emptiest heading available and says
nothing about the angle it lands on. #480's review blocked two drafts of a new
caption on exactly that ground, which is how the old sentence became a question
instead of a fixture.

Measured. It holds, and by more than the caption asked for:

    seed   sys  bodies  halfDiag   nearest        limb    verdict
      47  248       5     49.66°  ERARLE      159.58°  out by 109.91°
       4  206      15     49.66°  REMALE V     89.81°  out by  40.15°
      23  178       4     49.66°  RAANOR I    166.45°  out by 116.79°

Eleven worlds, headed ANGLE Metal, framed by the shipped `deepSky` placement.
The tightest of them keeps the nearest **limb** — the subject's disc, `asin(r/d)`,
not its centre — 89.81° off the view axis against a frame whose widest angle is
49.66°. Nothing is merely outside the cone: on every one of those worlds every
subject is behind the camera's plane. So the two words stand.

What that obliges is the part that can be held, and this is the whole of it:

- `scripts/measure-sky-clearance.mjs` sweeps eleven seeds and prints the table.
  It asserts nothing and is EXEMPT from the launcher-wiring tally for the same
  reason #206's and #201's instruments are: the judgement is the probe's, and a
  table that also gated would be the check deriving itself from its own subject.
- `scripts/lib/sky-clearance.mjs` is the one owner of the arithmetic, with two
  consumers. A sweep and a gate each carrying their own copy of an angle is how a
  gate goes false-green while the instrument keeps printing a confident table.
- `probe-sky.mjs` holds one world: the sheet's own seed, imported rather than
  pinned by number, because that is the only world `sky-only` is ever drawn in.
  It does not claim the spread's authority for a single boot.

The row was checked for biting rather than believed: aimed at the star, the same
reading reports the whole system in frame (6 subjects, margin -49.77°), so the
predicate is not vacuous.

Three things the reading cannot see, and the comment says rather than implies.
Bloom spreads outside a disc and is #98's lever, not geometry. The canopy covers
part of the rectangle, so every figure here is conservative. And the camera looks
down its own -Z: read that column without negating it and every subject lands
roughly ahead, the clearance reads as a fraction of itself, and the instrument is
wrong with total confidence — which is why the sign is written out in the module
that owns it.

No figure is reprinted in `view-set.mjs`. The `deep-sky` kind note says where the
number lives instead, because this file has carried a stale one before, and the
strongest word on a caption should be held by a row rather than by a comment.
