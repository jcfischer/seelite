## Drum priorities

1. Make the endcap cross continuous and structural. The vertical dark backing still stops short of the bay, leaving pale gaps; horizontal vent boxes also read as separate appliques. Join substantial dark channels from rim to bay surround, with visible pipes and clamps inside them. The target cross is a major organizing feature.
2. Darken and articulate the broad front bevel. Its upper-left sector is still pale and its lower-right sector largely featureless. Target facets are dark armor with seams, rim hardware and varying small panels.
3. Break up the broad empty dark axial stripe above the middle machinery lane. Add recessed sidewalls and equipment across its width, rather than repeating another line of similar vent blocks along its lower edge.
4. Improve bay depth and light distribution. The left wall is a bright flat patch and the interior reads shallow; target has a clear ceiling, receding wall divisions, and lit floor. Reduce the large white corner fixtures and distribute smaller amber sources.
5. Introduce sharper local metallic highlights and surface hierarchy. Current mottled wear is plausible, but the pale plates, framing and machinery largely share the same rough diffuse response.

## Assembly priorities

1. Give the terminal its dark segmented perimeter and front-face cross straps. Two vent assemblies improve the blank barrel, but most visible pale area remains unframed; the target terminal reads as an armored capsule with layered collars and amber fixtures.
2. Move the lower service pod farther inward along the truss. It remains partly hidden by the tank rather than visibly centered in the connecting span. Trim the unnecessary truss extension beyond the tank. Expose more upper service span as well.
3. Replace radiator center gold squares with narrow perimeter edges and four or five slim dark fin panels. All three radiator shapes still differ conspicuously from the target.
4. Light the mast junction boxes with small amber lamps and add edge hardware. Current dark cubes lack the target serviced junctions and visual rhythm.
5. Resolve the hangar's large dark triangular region with coherent ceiling and wall surfaces. Warm light is present, but the target reads as a deep rectangular room.
6. Refine axial collars and reposition the hanging tank a little farther right. The target has a more articulated, spaced chain of framed modules; the current right-hand sequence remains visually coarse.

## Shared

The sparse, uniform starfield and lack of tiny scale cues remain secondary fidelity gaps. Retain the established silhouette and framing while fixing the large visible surface structures above. More repeated vents alone will not address those gaps.
