**Below the 7.0 threshold.** Round 7 adds visible lower-body equipment, darker lower framing and more substantial dark vertical corner structure. These are useful density changes, but do not resolve the principal target gaps or justify a category increase at tenth-point precision. The lower equipment also makes the base busier while the broad right upper cylinder remains comparatively empty. Lighting and surface character are effectively unchanged.

Priority corrections:

1. Replace the pale pointed supports under the roof with continuous dark diagonal braces joining the roof frame to the piers. The new dark vertical blocks provide mass but do not supply the target's diagonal load-bearing silhouette. Add the curved paired service pipes immediately beside the front/right pier.
2. Rebuild the large hatch as a chamfered recessed assembly with layered collars. Its broad pale rectangular border and nearly flat central face remain dominant. Shorten side/bottom lights and make a compact top fixture illuminate the assembly; the current central amber patch still resembles a spotlight on a slab.
3. Rotate the bottom radiator until its ribbed face is visible, and give it a dark border and substantial hinge root. It still presents a pale edge-on wedge instead of the target's hanging dark panel.
4. Break the roof's plain flat perimeter into layered rails, inset service channels and varied corner attachments. Widen/lower the hut and reduce the four large antenna lamps. Weather the uninterrupted pad lines.
5. Replace uniform small dirt speckling with larger plate-value variation, edge chips and seam staining. Recess equipment into the right cylinder so the intermediate scale has mechanical depth rather than more surface-mounted vent boxes.

The target has richer fixture-led amber accents, clearer local shadow depth and a denser starfield. Neither extra lower boxes nor overall brightness alone will address those differences. This judgment does not substitute for principal visual approval.
