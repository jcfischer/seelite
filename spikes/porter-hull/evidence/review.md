## Final judge verdict

Round 7: 7.1/10, a modest improvement over 6.9.

Roof armor and reduced glazing better integrate the cockpit. Finer wear looks less blotchy. Warm spill adds depth to machinery, though its broad orange pools are stronger than the target’s restrained service lights.

Remaining gaps:
- Target’s blue-gray command hull contrasts clearly with ivory cargo; current hull remains predominantly uniform gray.
- Target has denser, smaller panel detailing, layered recesses, exposed connections, and varied mechanical forms. Repeated large hatches and identical pipe assemblies remain conspicuous.
- Current cockpit is still tall and angular; target cockpit sits within a more sculpted, heavily armored nose.
- Broad surfaces lack the target’s convincing metallic highlights and concentrated edge wear.
- Target fills more vertical space and has a richer starfield.
