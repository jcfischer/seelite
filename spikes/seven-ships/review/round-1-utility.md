## Raider

- **Framing:** Target fills approximately 92% of image width and 79% of height; capture about 63% and 46%. Increase projected ship size roughly 1.4x and modestly increase camera elevation so the upper surfaces approach the target's steep diagonal presentation. Preserve margins.
- **Hull volume:** Current center is a broad, uninterrupted flat slab. Target has a raised, narrower center spine, stepped shoulder armor, recessed engineering channels, and separate full-length forward flank pods. Lower and narrow the connecting deck, build a substantial raised spine, and cut dark slots between it and the flank structures. Do not merely emboss the current deck.
- **Nose:** Target ends in a broad, blunt armored single face. Current nose is thin and visibly split into two projecting tips. Join the tip and add depth; carry shoulder armor further forward and shorten the bare tapered run. Add a substantial raised cockpit frame with a forward lower glazing panel.
- **Engines:** Keep the rear twin engines, but target pods are bulkier vertically with clearer mechanical necks. Separate engines from central structure with exposed pipes rather than a solid connecting roof.
- **Weapons:** Current tiny boxes and short paired tubes do not communicate target armament. Increase mounts and barrel assemblies about 2–3x in visible volume, extend barrels forward, add stepped sleeves and cylindrical breeches, and mount them along substantial forward shoulder pods.
- **Lighting:** Reduce the isolated bright center-deck specular spot; use a broad upper-left key and lower fill to create a stronger bright-top/dark-side hierarchy. Target has many small warm work lamps with restrained bloom; add these at shoulder corners, pod faces and nose, not as a global exposure boost.
- **Materials:** Target is predominantly dark graphite with selectively worn cream and rust-red plates. Current large pale/rose panels dominate. Darken the base, reduce painted area, use varied smaller plates and realistic edge chipping; reduce uniform black outlines that make every panel look drawn. Give pipework and engine bands a distinct metallic response.
- **Details:** Replace repeated hatch stamps with varied recessed vents, panel seams, service doors, pipes, bent conduits and fastening rows placed around actual joints. Target detail is dense at structure transitions, not evenly tiled across flat roofs.

## Prospector

- **Framing:** Target occupies approximately 91% width and 79% height; capture 56% and 48%. Increase projected size about 1.55x. Target's front equipment drops farther below the cockpit; retain enough lower margin for it.
- **Core structure:** Current ship has a nearly naked black cylindrical backbone, long exposed rods and evenly spaced white blocks on top. Target is a tightly integrated chain of armored machinery with interrupted negative spaces. Cover most of the backbone with offset cream/yellow rectangular machinery casings, dark collars and side mechanical modules; replace the regular white rooftop blocks with contiguous stepped spine armor.
- **Pod mass:** Current pods are too shallow and have repetitive full-side grille strips. Increase their depth/height, bevel their ends more heavily and fit dark endcaps with central mechanical connectors. Use broad painted side panels punctuated by smaller discrete vents; maintain the recognizable two rear and two middle pods.
- **Cabin:** Enlarge and raise the cockpit module, add its forward lower windshield and broad armored cheek/side structures. Target cab looks like an inhabited industrial module; current one is a small wedge on the end of a pipe. Thicken its neck into the spine and add under-cabin corrugated hoses.
- **Drill:** Current small smooth cylinder with a rounded end fails the role-defining target. Increase drill diameter and mount mass, extend its ribbed motor, add several descending conical cutter rings toward a pointed tip, a large toothed/perforated chuck ring, and a visible three/four-prong protective cradle. The drill must read clearly even without texture.
- **Lighting:** Preserve upper-left illumination but use broader highlights and darker recesses under/between machinery. Add warm working lights at module junctions and front cabin, retaining cool sensor/glass accents.
- **Materials:** Yellow/cream palette is recognized, but target has large cream bands against ochre, dark steel endcaps and separated pipe materials. Current cream is heavily stained while yellow is comparatively flat. Balance wear across both colors, soften painted seam outlines, and add bevel highlights and roughness variation instead of relying on noise.
- **Details:** Add bundles of short connecting pipes and piston assemblies between pods and spine, varied radiator grilles, brackets, a sturdier two-column sensor mast and side machinery below the spine. Remove the evenly spaced identical white chimney-like blocks.
