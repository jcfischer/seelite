## Verified repairs and their limits

- Both patrol and raider loose nose strips are gone. The grille below each canopy is now unobstructed, and there is no plank bridging into the windshield. This specific defect is resolved visually. The patrol target's broad blue nose marking is now absent again; restoring it requires paint on hull-conforming panels, not a floating plate.
- Canopy side wings no longer project over the hull. Solid armor cheeks surround a narrower frontal pane. This fixes the awkward projecting-glass appearance. However, the result is a small rectangular skylight on a largely unchanged wedge, not the target's shaped occupied cabin with two differently angled glazing stages. Prospector particularly loses the substantial cab focal point.
- Gun bases remain visibly seated in shoulder structures in detail/top captures. Barrels enter their gun bodies. No unambiguous detached gun or grille assembly is visible in these supplied views. This is image evidence, not a full mesh-connectivity proof.
- Metal edges now read harder and more deliberate; bases have less of the earlier soft rubber-plinth appearance. Yet their silhouettes remain rectangular blocks, and some surfaces now read as especially plain black boxes.
- Relocated light gives clearer cast shadows and brighter cool edge highlights. This improves depth readability in places, but front grilles become nearly black and blue-white highlights pull away from the target's warm industrial response. It does not repair the missing recess geometry or warm local illumination.

## Interceptor — 5.50

The round-6 center-module improvement survives. Remaining score blockers are unchanged: identical repeated cream-topped center housings, oversized square nacelle front screens, a simple wedge cabin, missing broad blue cabin marking, little medium-scale mechanical variation and weak warm utility lighting. The narrow pane is better attached but still lacks the target's framed compound cockpit. Give the center units different lengths/sections, recess machinery in their flanks, build smaller beveled pod endcaps around inset grilles, and construct the cabin's two-stage solid/glass geometry. No overall target-similarity gain from this pass.

## Patrol — 5.10

Removal of the loose stripe is a correctness gain. The main body remains a blunt, long tiled slab with abruptly terminated side cheeks. Raised center equipment and gun plinths sit on it rather than defining the target's layered hull. The target's deep nacelle machinery channels, broad blue bands, sectional sensor dome and integrated sloped gun casings are still absent. Build a continuous tapered forebody, excavate the channels and place weapon mount volumes within the shoulders. Replace the polished unsegmented dome and glowing equator with armor sectors, dark glazing band and local indicators. Cosmetic cleanup does not outweigh the persistent composition/material mismatch.

## Raider — 5.00

No loose strip and no visibly floating gun; those repairs hold. Target remains graphite-dominant and mechanically dense; render remains red-dominant with a regular checkerboard skin. The required three forward masses and deep intervening channels have not appeared. Guns remain high-mounted blocks and uniform tubes, instead of low integrated breeches with stepped barrels. Current narrow flat canopy still lacks the target's raised armored compound cab. Rebuild those masses and their interfaces before further lighting or trim work. The extra contrast emphasizes the existing slab and grid rather than concealing it.

## Prospector — 5.25

The fuller modular spine remains an improvement over round 3, but it still repeats the interceptor's modules. Cabin still has no distinct upper occupied compartment over a lower chassis; the smaller rectangular pane makes that missing cabin hierarchy more apparent. Drill is still a short blunt cylinder assembly with a broad skid, not a long descending pointed cutter in a separated claw cradle. Blank mid-pod end faces, repeated center roofs, plain cube sensor and loose hero framing remain. A real upper-cab/chassis split and longer pointed cutter are essential silhouette changes, not optional extra detail. The small reduction reflects weaker cabin resemblance, not a general rendering failure.

All four remain approximately halfway to the approved reference fidelity. Round-6 architectural guidance still applies, but the repeated same-score result means the autonomous visual loop has reached its stop-and-consult condition. Show the principal current heroes together with targets and these honest scores; do not label the fleet visually approved or target-matched.
