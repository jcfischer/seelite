# Asset provenance — Codex

Original Bollard geometry authored in Blender. No third-party model downloaded. Fal credential names were absent from the process and local environment file. Blender authoring follows the Dream Loop Pro fallback and the principal's instruction to follow Courier and Coriolis workflows. The reusable authoring helpers, viewer and capture scaffolding came from the Courier O4 study at node 795, observed commit 0856987.

The principal selected O4: roughly three parts O2 Salvage (open frame, ivory cabin and twin engine pods), one part O3 Heavy Pull (protective armour and reinforced collar). The exact target prompt is copied in `target-prompt.txt`. Earlier concepts were grounded in a live screenshot of the procedural Bollard, the selected Courier exterior and the cockpit/Coriolis reference.

## Active paint texture

`paint-wear.png` was generated with built-in imagegen on 2026-10-08, output 1254 × 1254. The GLB embeds it. Material tints and roughness are authored separately; the albedo also supplies a subtle viewer bump. This is generated albedo, not a measured PBR material set.

Prompt:

Generate one square tileable PBR base-color texture for an authored weathered industrial salvage tug model. Flat orthographic evenly unlit material scan of old warm ivory enamel paint on steel. Mostly intact pale grey-ivory paint, with localized irregular charcoal-grey exposed-metal chips, dragged scratches, small scuffed clusters and oily grey smudges. Vary wear size: fine scratches plus several readable chipped patches 15-40 pixels across at 1024 pixels. About 75 percent clean intact painted area, 15 percent worn exposed grey metal, 10 percent fine scratches and grime. Organized random localized wear, not an even speckle everywhere. Real shipyard machinery material, tactile painted metal. NO panel seams, rivets, bolts, objects, shadows, highlights, gradients, text, symbols, perspective, rust clouds, stone or concrete noise. This is albedo only; geometry supplies panels and hardware. Seamless edges. Neutral grey values so the 3D material can tint it ivory or ochre.
