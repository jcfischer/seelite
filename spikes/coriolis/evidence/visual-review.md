## Materials gaps

- White panels retain essentially uniform brown mottling. At this scale it reads as porous stained stone rather than painted metal. Reduce broad all-over mottling, vary plate base values subtly, and concentrate streaks at seams/bolts with sharp chips and worn edges.
- Target panels have overlapping layers, local inset strips, joints and substantial bolts. Current rectangular tiles are thin and regular. Give selected large plates different thicknesses and overlaps; add sparse fastener clusters that remain legible at hero scale.
- Structural beams need assembled covers, seams, interrupted bevels and localized hazard paint. The rough chipped outer outline and noise texture do not provide the target's construction hierarchy.
- Entrance side columns are a useful addition but have repeated rectangular bays with little identifiable equipment. Distinguish steel pipes, dark hoses, painted electrical boxes, brackets and worn metal lips, with roughness and color differences rather than a shared dirty texture.
- Radiators need deeper recesses, visible frame thickness, mounting clamps, fine dense fins and surrounding service access. Current bay fronts and surrounding darkness make them read as attached flat grilles.
- Target '03' is larger, broad and stencil-like, with weathering and a prominent worn yellow hazard patch below. Current thin clean numerals and absent adjacent patch still mismatch.
- Hazard markings should also appear selectively on beam joints and service edges. Keep them weathered and low-saturation rather than uniformly clean entrance stripes.

## Detail gaps

- Long horizontal rail-like lines need explicit decks, upright rails, support triangles and occasional ladders. Their current shape remains ambiguous between pipes and walkways.
- Repeated small black rectangular vents appear at arbitrary regular locations; replace some with different functionally legible assemblies and integrate remaining vents into framed panels.

- Improve thin-line antialiasing after geometry is settled. Fins and railings show jagged/pixel-stepped detail.
