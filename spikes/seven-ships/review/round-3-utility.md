Verdict: modest improvement, but the architectural pass has largely stalled against the approved target. Neither is close to passing. Incremental additions have improved readability without replacing the structural vocabulary responsible for the mismatch.

## Raider

**Improved:** New forward cheeks shorten the visually bare nose and make the hull feel wider and sturdier. Gun barrels are more visible. Reduced side grille repetition on rear housings is welcome. Silhouette is more credible than round 2.

**Unresolved:** Main body still reads as a terraced slab with armor added to its edges. The target's defining dark channels between a raised central spine and heavy forward side structures remain absent. Long straight exposed rails visually bridge over the required cavities. Guns remain plain blocks with two short straight tubes, just raised farther above the hull. Target's long segmented barrels, supported breeches and integration into side pods are missing. The huge simple engine pod front grille remains too dominant compared with the target's layered rim/endcap. Target is graphite-dominant with selective paint; current image is red-dominant and the center still has a checkerboard pattern. Lighting and surface response have essentially stalled.

**Required next change:** Rebuild the center cross-section, not its accessories. Narrow and raise the central spine; lower the support deck so open channels run longitudinally between it and the two forward flanks. Use the flanks as gun foundations. Replace each entire gun with a rear housing, cylindrical breech, long paired barrels, barrel sleeves and mount rails. Short bent connections should cross the channels at a few locations; remove the full-length upper rail. Rebalance material by assigning graphite to most structural armor and red/cream to a few continuous plate regions. Make the engine front a thick beveled frame with an inset grille, rather than a flat grille bordered by thin trim.

Canopy still lacks the target's two-stage forward windshield and heavy frame. Add solid framed cabin geometry around glass rather than enlarging the pane. Warm work lamps and more directional highlights can help after the spatial hierarchy exists.

## Prospector

**Improved:** Enlarged cabin volume is helpful and glazing appears better seated. Rear pod cream grouping is more coherent. Curved spine pipes show some effort to break the perfectly straight layout.

**Unresolved:** The same overly long, thin central barrel remains plainly visible. Four large hoops around that barrel emphasize its tubular skeleton instead of producing the target's dense machinery chain. Small vents still repeat along the top at nearly equal intervals. Mid pods and cabin are too far apart; the target is a more tightly coupled chain of full-depth machinery. Cabin is still a long monolithic wedge rather than a boxy occupied upper cabin over a separate armored lower chassis. It lacks two-tier glazing. Drill end remains blunt and cylindrical, with the target's pointed stepped cutter silhouette absent. Camera framing remains too loose. Added hoops and small side devices have not solved these defining gaps.

**Required next change:** Shorten the middle exposed run by roughly one third, deepen the machinery there, and replace most of the cylinder/hoops with 3–4 distinct rectangular chamfered machinery bodies. Each should have its own shape and connection; pipe bundles should occupy short local gaps rather than form an external cage. Thicken the pod endcaps and put piston/collar connections into their faces. Split the cabin into a boxy upper cabin and lower forward chassis with side cheeks, then inset upper and lower windshield panes. Replace the drill tip with descending conical cutter stages terminating at a point; enlarge its toothed chuck and use a substantial multi-prong cradle. Scale the hero image up approximately 20–25%, retaining space around the drill.

## Shared cause and next-round acceptance checks

Current housings still share the same generic chamfered-box, rectangular panel lattice and flat hatch vocabulary. Targets depend on an intermediate scale between the outer hull and tiny trim: thick frames, stepped machinery castings, deep joints, inset grilles, collars, cab armor and layered weapon/drill assemblies. Most work is still at either hull-envelope or decoration scale.

Before another detail pass, inspect a single-gray render. It should visibly contain:

1. Raider: three distinct forward longitudinal masses with deep channels; complete long gun assemblies; graphite-first armor regions in the final material view.
2. Prospector: compact deep machinery chain; clearly separate cabin/chassis; pointed drill silhouette.
3. Both: thick rim profiles, recessed rather than surface-stamped components, and a clear bright-top/dark-recess lighting hierarchy.

Without those changes, adding more vents, lights or pipes is unlikely to move scores materially.
